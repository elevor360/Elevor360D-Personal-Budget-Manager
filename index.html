How buying works

Before buying: Please read the app details and installation requirements. Payments are handled by Payhip, not this website.

Choose your app and click Buy Now. You'll be taken to a secure Payhip checkout. After successful payment, Payhip provides a download link and sends purchase information by email. Keep your receipt and follow the app-specific instructions. If an app requires activation, its listing or download instructions will explain the process.

Our five paid apps are installable web apps (PWAs). Payhip provides a customer installation PDF, and your personal licence key arrives separately by email. Activate once online on up to two devices, then use the installed app offline.

Customer Help & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive an app after purchasing?

Select Buy Now to purchase securely through Payhip. Download the installation PDF, open its app link and use the personal licence key sent separately by email.

Can I install apps on Android, tablets or Windows?

Many apps are installable web apps (PWAs). Installation varies by browser and device. Follow the instructions supplied with each app; some require web hosting before installation.

Are the cruise and Local Deals apps free?

Yes. Use Open Free App to visit the relevant app. Browser installation is available where supported.

What if I need help with installation?

Use our contact form with the app name, device and a description of the problem.

Can I share or resell a paid app?

Paid apps are supplied for the purchaser’s use under the terms provided with the product. Contact us about business or multiple-user licensing.
