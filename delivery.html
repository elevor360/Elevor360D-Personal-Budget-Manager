App Delivery & Installation

1. Before you buy

Check the app description and supported devices. Most Elevor360D products are progressive web apps (PWAs), not conventional Play Store or Windows installer apps. Our listed paid and free apps are hosted online and do not require you to upload files to a hosting service. Product-specific instructions take priority.

2. Secure checkout

When a paid product is available, the Buy Now link takes you to Payhip. Payment is completed on Payhip’s checkout; Elevor360D does not collect your card number.

3. Receive your files

After successful payment, use Payhip’s download link and check your purchase email. Save your installation PDF and receipt. Your personal licence key arrives in a separate email. If you cannot access the download, contact support with the app name and your purchase reference (never send card details).

4. Install your app

  1. Download the installation PDF, open the app link and enter the licence key emailed separately. Initial activation requires internet access.
  2. If required, extract and publish the app files on compatible HTTPS hosting.
  3. Open the app URL in Chrome or another supported browser.
  4. On Android, use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen where offered. On Windows, look for the browser's install option.
  5. If activation is required, follow the product's activation instructions.

Browser menus vary by device; installation availability is not guaranteed on every browser.

5. Help

Email elevor360@gmail.com with the app name, device, browser and issue.
