How to buy, activate & install your app

Follow these simple steps to start using your Elevor360D app.

1. Choose your app

Browse the store and select Details to read the features and installation information. Free apps can be opened directly without checkout.

2. Buy securely through Payhip

For paid apps, click Buy Now. Checkout takes place on Payhip. After payment, download the installation PDF provided with your purchase.

Keep your purchase confirmation for reference.

3. Open your app

Follow the link and instructions in your installation PDF.

4. Activate your paid app

Your licence key is sent separately by email. Enter it in the app when prompted. Each purchased app includes one activation licence, which can be used to install and activate that app on up to two devices. Use the same licence code on both devices. A third device cannot be activated with that licence. For activation help, please use our Contact Us form.

You may need an internet connection for initial activation. Some apps are designed for offline use after activation; check each app's details for its specific requirements.

5. Install on your device

  • Android / Chrome: Open the app link in Chrome. Use the browser menu and select Install app or Add to Home screen when offered.
  • Windows / Chrome or Edge: Open the app link and use the install icon in the address bar, if shown, or the browser's app installation option.
  • Other devices: Installation options depend on your browser and device. The app may still run in a browser even when installation is unavailable.

Need help?

If you cannot find your licence email, have trouble activating, or need help installing, contact us using the website contact form. Include the app name and your purchase email address, but never send passwords or payment card details.
